[RULES]
G1 Extract every piece of personal or sensitive information of the supplied types. Return exact spans from the text.
G2 The text can be prose, a form or a data record (JSON, XML, HTML, SQL, a table or a log). Label the values only, never the field labels, keys, tags or column names in front of them.
G3 Words that only name a kind of data, such as "social security number", "medical record number", "customer ID", "license plate", "date of birth" or "password", are labels, also in the middle of a sentence. Never extract them; extract only the actual value.

[TYPE] PERSON
[DESCRIPTION] A person's first name, last name or full name, with middle names and initials.
PE1 "signed by Maria L. Okafor on Friday" -> "Maria L. Okafor".
PE2 "First Name: Imani" -> "Imani".
PE3 "'last_name': 'Okafor'" -> "Okafor".

[TYPE] TITLE
[DESCRIPTION] A title or honorific written with a person's name, such as "Mr", "Mrs", "Ms", "Miss", "Mx", "Dr" or "Prof".
TI1 "Dear Dr. Imani Okafor" -> "Dr." (and "Imani Okafor" is PERSON).

[TYPE] USERNAME
[DESCRIPTION] A username or login handle of a person, usually lower case with dots, underscores or digits, such as "t.okafor92" or "rosa_diaz".
UN1 "Username: t.okafor92" -> "t.okafor92".

[TYPE] EMAIL
[DESCRIPTION] An email address, with its @ sign.
EM1 "write to rosa.diaz@example.org" -> "rosa.diaz@example.org".
EM2 "Email: t.okafor@example.org" -> "t.okafor@example.org".

[TYPE] PHONE
[DESCRIPTION] The digits of a phone or fax number, with its country code, separators and extension.
PH1 "call +44 20 7946 0321" -> "+44 20 7946 0321".
PH2 "Phone: 555-0142 ext. 7" -> "555-0142 ext. 7".

[TYPE] ADDRESS
[DESCRIPTION] A street address, starting at its house number or street name: house number and street, with the flat, town, state, postcode and country when they are written next to it, as one span.
AD1 "lives at 48 Harbour Road, Flat 2, Leeds LS6 2QX" -> "48 Harbour Road, Flat 2, Leeds LS6 2QX".
AD2 "'secondary_address': 'Suite 364'" -> "Suite 364".

[TYPE] LOCATION
[DESCRIPTION] A city, town, county, state, province, region or country, written out or as a code such as "TX" or "US"; also a postcode or ZIP code.
LO1 "the office in Leeds will close" -> "Leeds".
LO2 "State: Ohio" -> "Ohio".
LO3 "'country_code': 'PT'" -> "PT".
LO4 "Postcode: LS6 2QX" -> "LS6 2QX".

[TYPE] COORDINATE
[DESCRIPTION] The decimal number of a latitude or longitude, such as "53.8008", or a pair of them.
CO1 "<latitude>53.8008</latitude>" -> "53.8008".
CO2 "found at 53.8008, -1.5491" -> "53.8008, -1.5491".
CO3 "{'latitude': '53.8008', 'longitude': '-1.5491'}" -> "53.8008" and "-1.5491", never the keys.

[TYPE] ORGANIZATION
[DESCRIPTION] The name of a company, employer, bank, school, hospital, agency or other organization.
OR1 "an engineer at Hartwell & Finch Ltd" -> "Hartwell & Finch Ltd".

[TYPE] OCCUPATION
[DESCRIPTION] A person's job, job title or profession; also their level of education or employment status.
OC1 "works as a dental hygienist" -> "dental hygienist".
OC2 "'occupation': 'Aircraft pilot'" -> "Aircraft pilot".
OC3 "holds a Master's degree and is currently self-employed" -> "Master's degree" and "self-employed".

[TYPE] DATE_TIME
[DESCRIPTION] A calendar date in any format, including a date of birth; a time of day; a timestamp with both; also a weekday, month or year given as a value.
DA1 "born on 30th June 1985" -> "30th June 1985".
DA2 "Date of Birth: 1984-03-09" -> "1984-03-09".
DA3 "logged at 2023-04-11T09:15:00Z" -> "2023-04-11T09:15:00Z".
DA4 "'week_day': 'Thursday'" -> "Thursday".
DA5 "at 7:30 AM the alarm rang" -> "7:30 AM".

[TYPE] AGE
[DESCRIPTION] A person's age: the number only.
AG1 "a 47-year-old applicant" -> "47".
AG2 "(Age: 63, Sex: F)" -> "63".
AG3 "she is 58 years old" -> "58".

[TYPE] GENDER
[DESCRIPTION] The value given for a person's gender or sex, such as "female", "M" or "Non-binary".
GE1 "(Age: 63, Sex: F)" -> "F".
GE2 "Gender: Non-binary" -> "Non-binary".

[TYPE] DEMOGRAPHIC

[TYPE] IP_ADDRESS

[TYPE] URL
[DESCRIPTION] A web address or domain name, complete with its path and query string.

[TYPE] FINANCIAL

[RULES]
G4 A field label, key or tag name is never an entity; only the value written after it is. In "Date of Birth: 1984-03-09" label "1984-03-09" and nothing else; in "{'contact_email': 't.okafor@example.org'}" label "t.okafor@example.org" and nothing else. This overrides everything above.
